What’s New

Release notes.

Each version of this website is named after a place in Kenya, in alphabetical order. You are viewing Fourteen Falls (version 1.5.0).

  1. F

    Fourteen Falls 1.5.0

    16 November 2026 · A wide waterfall on the Athi River near Thika.

    • Colours follow the brand hierarchy: orange for main buttons, periwinkle for links and icons.
    • Section labels use the logo typeface, and stay neat when they wrap on phones.
  2. E

    Elgon 1.4.0

    16 November 2026 · A mountain on the border with Uganda, known for its caves.

    • Services organised as four pillars, including the new Workforce Technology & Transformation.
    • Three quick starting points: Recruit Talent in Kenya, Build a Remote Team and Establish Workforce Operations in Kenya.
    • A page for each pillar, with its supporting services beneath it.
    • The enquiry form asks for the roles, number of people, location and start date.
  3. D

    Diani 1.3.0

    16 November 2026 · A white sand beach on Kenya’s south coast.

    • A new home page opening: “Build and manage high-performing teams in Kenya.”
    • Serif headings and typewriter style section labels across the site.
    • Photography that mostly shows African professionals, with every photo used once.
    • Shorter vision and mission cards on About Us.
  4. C

    Chalbi 1.2.0

    8 November 2026 · A desert of salt flats in northern Kenya.

    • Anonymous visit statistics with no cookies, explained in the Privacy Policy.
  5. B

    Baringo 1.1.0

    8 November 2026 · A freshwater lake in the Rift Valley.

    • Faster pages, sharper link previews and better search results.
    • A floating return to top button.
    • Accessibility fixes for contrast and links.
  6. A

    Amboseli 1.0.0

    8 November 2026 · A national park beneath Mount Kilimanjaro.

    • First release of the Avenyra Global website.

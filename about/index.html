About Avenyra Global

A people-centred workforce partner.

Avenyra Global works with organisations that need practical human resource support, and with people looking for work that suits their skills.

Based in NairobiServing Kenya and beyond
Who We Are

Flexible, dependable HR support.

People are the foundation of every successful organisation. Managing a workforce requires more than filling positions; it requires the right people, effective processes and professional human resource support.

Avenyra Global is a human resource outsourcing, recruitment and workforce solutions company. The firm works with businesses, institutions, government organisations, NGOs and people seeking employment.

People. Potential. Progress.
Our Vision

Workplaces where people can do good work.

Organisations in which people are supported properly, and workforce decisions are made with care, clarity and a record that stands up to review.

Our Mission

Practical support for people and organisations.

To provide human resource outsourcing, recruitment and workforce solutions that help organisations attract capable people, manage them well and pursue their objectives.

Our Core Values

Six values behind every engagement.

How clients, candidates and employees should experience working with us.

01

Integrity

We say what the work includes, keep confidence where it is required, and do not overstate what we can do.

02

Professionalism

Clients and candidates should experience orderly communication, careful records and a respectful manner.

03

People First

The person affected by a decision is kept in view, whether they are a hiring manager, an employee or a candidate.

04

Excellence

Routine work is done properly. Advice is considered before it is given.

05

Innovation

We look for clearer ways to handle workforce administration without adding process for its own sake.

06

Collaboration

Good workforce outcomes depend on working with managers, employees and partner organisations.
